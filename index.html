About Me

I am an Assistant Research Fellow at Peng Cheng Laboratory in Shenzhen, China. My research interests span computer vision, pattern recognition, and spectrum sensing — with a focus on radio map estimation, 3D point cloud recognition, knowledge distillation, and fine-grained visual classification.

I received my Ph.D. from the South China University of Technology in 2023, after which I joined Peng Cheng Laboratory as a Postdoc (2023–2025) and was promoted to Assistant Research Fellow in 2025. I collaborate closely with researchers from Peking University, CUHK-Shenzhen, and HITSZ.

Research Interests

📡 Radio Map Estimation & Spectrum Sensing

Physics-inspired deep learning models for constructing radio maps from extremely sparse spatial observations. Applications in spectrum sensing, cognitive radio networks, and low-altitude airspace 3D radio map estimation.

🧊 3D Point Cloud Recognition

Topology-aware modeling and unsupervised simulation-to-reality transfer for 3D point cloud understanding, including structured architectural abstraction and single-view object classification.

🧠 Representation Learning & Distillation

Improving deep representations via knowledge distillation, auxiliary learnable target coding, data augmentation (ShuffleMix), and large-small model collaborative tuning.

🔒 AI Security & Robustness

Defending against similarity-shift attacks for Embedding-as-a-Service (EaaS) via adaptive multi-target watermarking; adversarial robustness and test-time adaptation of 3D vision-language models.
